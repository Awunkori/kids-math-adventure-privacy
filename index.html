Privacy Policy for Kids Math Adventure Effective date: October 7, 2026 Kids Math Adventure is a child-friendly educational mathematics app designed to help children learn and practice early mathematics skills through interactive activities, narration, sounds, rewards, and progress-based gameplay. This Privacy Policy explains how Kids Math Adventure handles information when you use the app. 1. Information We Collect Kids Math Adventure is designed to minimize data collection. The app does not require users to create an account, sign in, or provide their name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or other personal information. The app may store game settings and learning progress locally on the device, such as selected audio and narration settings, captions and reduced-motion preferences, break-reminder settings, game progress, completed activities, rewards or achievement progress, and other gameplay preferences. This information is used only to support the app experience and is stored locally on the user's device unless otherwise stated in a future version of this policy. 2. Children's Privacy Kids Math Adventure is intended for children and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. The app does not require children to provide names, email addresses, account credentials, precise location, or other identifying information in order to play. Parents and guardians should review the app settings and supervise use as appropriate for the child. 3. Audio, Narration, and Text-to-Speech Kids Math Adventure may use device-based audio and text-to-speech functionality to provide narration, spoken instructions, music, and sound effects. These features are used to support learning and accessibility. The app does not use the microphone to record children and does not upload voice recordings to the developer. Some speech or text-to-speech functionality may be provided by the operating system or by a voice service installed on the device. Those services may be governed by the privacy terms of the device or service provider. 4. Device Permissions Kids Math Adventure is designed to request only permissions needed for app functionality. The app does not require access to precise location, contacts, call logs, SMS messages, camera, microphone recordings, photos or videos, or advertising identifiers unless a future version adds a feature that clearly requires such access. 5. Advertising Kids Math Adventure does not display third-party advertising in its current version. 6. Analytics and Tracking Kids Math Adventure does not use third-party advertising trackers or behavioral tracking technologies in its current version. 7. Data Sharing Kids Math Adventure does not sell personal information and does not share personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties. 8. Data Retention and Deletion Game progress and settings stored locally on the device may remain on the device until the user resets the app, clears the app's storage, uninstalls the app, or uses an in-app reset or deletion feature, if available. Because the current version does not maintain user accounts or a remote user database, there is generally no separate server-side personal account data for the developer to delete. 9. Security We take reasonable steps to design Kids Math Adventure in a way that minimizes the collection of personal information and limits access to information that is not needed for the app to function. 10. Third-Party Services The app may rely on platform services provided by Android, Google Play, device manufacturers, or installed text-to-speech/audio services in order to operate. Those services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies. 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes, when legal or platform requirements change, or when new features are added.
